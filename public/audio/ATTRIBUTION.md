# Jyoti fireworks audio credits

These three real field recordings are released under **CC0 1.0 / public domain dedication**. They are bundled locally; no third-party audio requests are made during playback.

- **Sparkler.aif**, Ned Bouhalassa. https://freesound.org/people/Ned%20Bouhalassa/sounds/8320/ — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  `sparkler-recording.wav`: excerpt at 10.0–14.5 seconds from the author's MP3 preview, converted to mono PCM at 22050 Hz. Used for Fuljhadi. The source describes a close-up stereo recording of a birthday sparkler.
- **Whistle and Explosion Single_Firework**, Rudmer_Rotteveel. https://freesound.org/people/Rudmer_Rotteveel/sounds/336008/ — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  `rocket-recording.mp3`: original HQ preview. Playback separates the recorded ascent (0–1.13s) from the explosion (1.14s onward); varies rate, level and pan. Used for rocket/whistling rocket, aerial shells, multi-shot and finale bursts.
- **Firecracker with wick #1**, Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank. https://bigsoundbank.com/firecracker-with-wick-1-s1137.html — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  `firecracker-recording.mp3`: original downloadable MP3, with short playback excerpts for the ladi. Used for ground cracker and ladi pops. Source: outdoor Tascam DR-40 + Sennheiser ME66 recording.

Licenses and source pages checked November 8, 2026. CC0 permits copying, editing, redistribution and commercial use. Attribution is retained voluntarily.

All additional fountain, spinner, lantern wind/flame, colour-fountain, rain, butterfly, fuse, thump and whistle layers are original procedurally synthesized **approximations**, generated by `src/fireworks/audio.js`. They are not authentic recordings of those individual fireworks. Recording load/decode failures use a designed fallback and are disclosed in the UI. No generic explosion is substituted for sustained fountain/spinner/lantern effects.

## Hand-held Fuljhadi upgrade

The same `sparkler-recording.wav` excerpt is looped for a 38-second burn with 25ms edge fades, gain envelopes, stereo positioning and a gradual burnout fade. Short excerpts around the recording’s strongest transient are used for occasional additional crackles/pops. These are transformations of the CC0 recording listed above. The 0.16-second match-strike ignition is original synthesized noise and is not represented as a field recording. A disclosed procedural sizzle is used only if the recording fails to load/decode. No new third-party sound or hand-model asset was added; the 3D hand is procedurally modeled in the source.
